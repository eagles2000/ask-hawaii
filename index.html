      Help prevent suicide in Hawaiʻi

      Ask Hawaiʻi is free. If it helped you, please consider a gift to Samaritan Counseling Center Hawaiʻi in Honolulu. Since 1989 it has counseled local families through grief, depression and suicidal thoughts, including faith when people want it, and its Client Assistance Fund helps those who can't afford care. Your donation goes straight to them.

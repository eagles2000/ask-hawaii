Use Ask Hawaiʻi inside your AI assistant

Add it to Claude (or any assistant that supports MCP connectors) and your assistant will check these facts automatically whenever you ask about Hawaiʻi. In Claude: Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector, then paste this address:

https://www.askaihawaii.com/api/mcp

Facts are checked against the sources shown on each answer, with the date they were last checked. Hours, fees and rules change, so confirm time-sensitive details on the official site. In an emergency, call 911.
